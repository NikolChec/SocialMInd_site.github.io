What it is

A gentle practice layer for social-emotional learning.

Counselors are stretched thin and many students struggle quietly. SocialMind gives schools a calm, structured way for every student to build social confidence - safely, and on their own time.

01 / STUDENTS

Practice without the pressure

Rehearse intimidating moments in private - and discover, one small step at a time, that they can do it.

02 / EDUCATORS

Extend your support, with care

An evidence-informed SEL tool that helps counselors reach far beyond what one-to-one time allows.

03 / SCHOOLS

Wellbeing you can stand behind

A modern, measurable program that shows your community you take belonging seriously.

Why it matters

Many students avoid the moments that would help them feel connected.

Many students don't speak up — not because they don't care, but because the moment feels overwhelming.

Over time, avoidance quietly becomes a habit, and students who look "fine" can struggle in silence. SocialMind turns those avoided moments into gentle, repeatable practice — so courage has somewhere safe to grow.

Schools need support tools that scale with care: structured practice, student dignity, and clear educator-friendly language.

How it works

From an avoided moment to a rehearsed skill.

Every short session blends an immersive scene with real-time AI - designed to build confidence safely and gradually.

STEP 01

Choose a scenario

Pick a real situation that usually feels stressful - from introductions to asking for help.

STEP 02

Step into the scene

Enter a realistic, calm school environment that feels real enough to matter, yet stays safe.

STEP 03

Talk with AI characters

Natural, real-time conversation with no scripts - and the freedom to retry as often as needed.

STEP 04

Reflect & set a goal

End with a short reflection and one small, kind micro-goal for next time.
