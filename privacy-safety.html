Privacy & Safety

Student dignity, privacy-aware development, and clear boundaries.

SocialMind is being developed with a high-trust posture for schools. Public claims stay high-level until technical practices are verified.

  • No diagnostic claims
  • Human judgment remains central
  • Detailed data practices need confirmation

Safety principles

Safety begins with how the product talks about students.

Student dignity

The product should avoid shame, labels, public ranking, or clinical framing.

Human oversight

Educators and school teams remain responsible for support decisions.

Bounded AI

AI supports practice and feedback direction; it should not make school decisions.

Privacy-aware development

SocialMind should minimize data needs and clarify practices before implementation.

  • Collect only what is needed for the agreed use case.
  • Discuss access, responsibilities, and safeguards before a pilot.
  • Document retention and deletion practices before launch.
  • Provide a clear privacy contact route.

What SocialMind does not do

Clear non-goals protect schools and students.

No diagnosis

SocialMind is not a diagnostic or clinical assessment tool.

No replacement for professionals

The product does not replace counselors, teachers, psychologists, or safeguarding teams.

No compliance claims yet

This website does not claim FERPA, COPPA, GDPR, ISO, encryption, or audit status without verification.
