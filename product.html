Product

A connected practice system for school social moments.

SocialMind is being developed as one connected experience: student practice, supportive AI feedback, reflection, developing educator insight, and an optional immersive practice layer.

  • Student practice comes first
  • Educators remain central
  • VR is optional, not the identity

Product overview

SocialMind gives students a structured place to rehearse realistic school situations before trying them in real life.

One connected experience

Practice, feedback, reflection, and developing educator support are designed to work together.

School-based scenarios

Situations focus on everyday moments such as starting a conversation, joining a group, asking for help, and participating in class.

Supportive boundaries

The product supports practice and reflection. It is not diagnostic and does not replace educators or counselors.

How the connected system works

The platform direction links student activity with a clear practice loop.

Student onboarding

Students provide initial context so practice can start from a relevant school situation.

Guided practice

Students rehearse conversation moments with AI characters in a safe, repeatable flow.

Feedback and reflection

After practice, the experience points students toward reflection and one small next step.

Student practice flow

Each session is designed to be simple, repeatable, and supportive.

Choose a scenario

A student chooses a realistic school social moment they want to practice.

Practice the moment

The student speaks naturally with AI characters that respond in real time.

Reflect and continue

The session ends with reflection and a small confidence-building next step.

Supportive AI feedback

Feedback is intended to be clear, kind, and useful for another attempt.

Strengths first

Feedback should help students notice what went well before naming what to try next.

Actionable next steps

Micro-goals keep progress small enough to practice.

Human oversight

Educator judgment stays central as the platform develops.

Developing educator support

SocialMind is being shaped toward educator-friendly insight without replacing adult judgment.

Practice patterns

Future educator views may help adults understand recurring practice patterns.

Progress direction

The product direction includes ways to observe practice over time.

Support conversations

Insights should support thoughtful school conversations, not automatic decisions.

Optional immersive practice

VR can add presence where it creates value, but SocialMind is not VR-first.

The optional immersive layer may support moments where realism helps students rehearse a situation more fully. Schools can still understand SocialMind as a practice and feedback system without treating VR as a requirement.

Product boundaries

Clear boundaries make the product safer and more credible.

  • SocialMind does not diagnose students.
  • SocialMind does not replace teachers, counselors, or mental-health professionals.
  • SocialMind does not guarantee outcomes.
  • SocialMind does not make automated school decisions.
